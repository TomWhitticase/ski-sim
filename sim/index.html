      Controls

      Keyboard and mouse

      Touch

      Left thumb
      Stick: steer; forward skates, back ploughs (drone, snowmobile, speed bar; the plane's stick and the helicopter's cyclic)
      Right side
      Drag to look around
      Buttons
      Jump, tuck and stop; flips in the air; up, down and speed for the drone; power, flaps and brake in the plane; up and down in the helicopter (a sideways drag is its pedals)
      Stop
      Snowplough; with the stick pushed left or right, a hockey stop that way
      Prompts
      Tap "Ride …" to board a lift, "Fly …" to climb into a parked aircraft, "Go to marker" to go there
      Tools
      Pause, map, drone, paraglider, snowmobile, view, labels, piste colours, fullscreen
      Map
      Pinch to zoom, tap for a marker, touch and hold to go there

      Plane

      E
      Beside a Porter parked at the top of a runway: climb in (it's the only way: walk, ski or ride to an airfield)
      U
      Get out, any time: stopped, beside it (it stays parked); rolling, onto your skis at its speed (it brakes and parks); in the air, a skydive
      W / S
      Power up / down; hold S at idle on the ground for reverse
      Mouse / arrows
      Stick: pull to climb, push to dive, roll to turn (centres when you let go; hands off levels out). The arrows ramp in and spring back; Fly aircraft with (above) sets the arrows alone, the mouse then only looking round
      A / D
      Roll; steer on the ground
      Q / E
      Rudder
      X
      Flaps: up, take-off, landing
      Space
      Brakes
      Right mouse
      Hold to look round the cockpit (V); the stick centres meanwhile

      Skydiving

      U
      In the air: jump out of the plane or helicopter (it flies on, then it's back at its airfield)
      A / D
      Turn (under the canopy: the toggles)
      W
      Track: glide forward about as fast as you fall
      S
      Flare: spread out and slow; under the canopy, both toggles down to land
      E / Q
      Head down (~290 km/h) / back to belly to earth (~195 km/h)
      Space
      Open the canopy (it opens itself at 300 m above the ground)
